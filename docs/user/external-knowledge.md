# External Knowledge: PDFs, The Signal, and The Sight

**TL;DR**: Transform consuming into creating by integrating papers, blogs, and videos directly into your permanent library.

## 1. PDF Highlighting & Annotation
Citadel includes a professional-grade **PDF Viewer** with specialized highlighting capabilities.

- **Persistent Highlights**: Highlight text in any PDF. Highlights are saved to your Keep and reappear when you reopen the file.
- **Audible Research**: Built-in **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** reads your papers back to you while you take notes.
- **Auto-Syncing Notes**: Your highlights are automatically synced to the associated scroll's note panel. Clicking a highlight jumps directly to that page in the PDF.
- **Local Storage**: PDFs are managed within your Keep, ensuring you never lose access to your sources.

## 2. The Signal (RSS Manager)
Stay updated without leaving your workspace.

- **Feed Aggregation**: Add your favorite blogs, research journals, or news sites.
- **One-Click Record**: Found an interesting article? Click "Record to Scroll" to save it into your **Keep** as a permanent Markdown record.
- **Offline Reading**: Once captured, you can read and annotate the article even without an internet connection.

## 3. The Sight (YouTube Manager)
Turn passive watching into active learning.

- **Mini-Player**: Watch videos in a corner of the app while you take notes in the main area.
- **Smart Conversion**: Found a video or article worth keeping? Click **Convert to Entry** to snapshot it into your permanent collection.
- **Timestamped Notes**: Create links that jump to specific moments in the video.
- **Organize by Channel**: Group your educational videos by topic or creator.

## 4. Web Discovery & Deep Linking
Citadel supports custom URL protocols (`citadel://`) and "clone" links.
- **Clone from Web**: If you see a Citadel-ready repo on the web, a single click can clone it into your local Keep.
- **Protocol Handlers**: Link to specific scrolls from external apps like your browser or task manager.
