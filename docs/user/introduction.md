# Introduction to Citadel

**TL;DR**: Citadel is a local-first, developer-centric "Second Brain" designed for high-performance knowledge management and engineering workflows.

Citadel isn't just another note-taking app. It’s a specialized workspace that combines the flexibility of Markdown with the power of integrated developer tools, all while keeping you in total control of your data.

## The Citadel Philosophy

### 1. Local-First, Always
Your knowledge belongs to you. Citadel stores everything as plain files on your local disk. There is no proprietary cloud, no database lock-in, and no hidden formats. If you can open a folder, you can use Citadel.

### 2. Git-Backed Synchronization
Instead of a proprietary sync engine, Citadel leverages **Git**. This means you get industrial-grade version history, branching, and collaboration for free. You can sync your "brain" using GitHub, GitLab, or any Git provider.

### 3. Developer-Centric Utilities
We believe your second brain should speak your language. Citadel integrates:
- **Monaco Editor**: The engine behind VS Code, for your snippets and scripts.
- **Integrated REPL**: Run code directly within your notes.
- **LaTeX Math**: Professional-grade mathematical typesetting.
- **Git Integration**: Commit and push changes without leaving the app.

### 4. Hackable Architecture
Citadel is designed to be shaped by you. Every workspace (we call them **Keeps**) can have its own unique configuration of entry types, folders, and modules.

## How It Works

Citadel organizes your knowledge into **Scrolls**. A scroll is a Markdown file enriched with **YAML Metadata**. 

- **The Archives**: Your command center for searching, filtering, and organizing scrolls.
- **The Scriptorium**: Specialized editors for Rich Text, Code, LaTeX, and Whiteboards.
- **The Media Hub**: RSS feeds (The Signal), YouTube managers (The Sight), and PDF highlighters that turn consumption into creation.
