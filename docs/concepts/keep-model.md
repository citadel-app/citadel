# Concept: The Keep Model

At its core, Citadel treats your data with a "Local-First" priority. This is achieved through the **Keep**.

## What is a Keep?
A Keep is simply a standard folder on your file system. Inside, Citadel stores your:
- **Scrolls**: Your notes, notebooks, and research.
- **Assets**: Images, PDFs, and other binary files.
- **Metadata Cache**: A hidden database that makes searching and filtering instantaneous.
- **The Bastion**: A `.git` folder that tracks every single change you make.

## The "Folder-as-Keep" Philosophy
Unlike cloud-based tools, Citadel does not "own" your data. 
- **Portability**: You can move your Keep folder anywhere, or open it in other editors like VS Code or Obsidian.
- **Zero Lock-In**: Because your scrolls are standard Markdown, your knowledge is never trapped in a proprietary format.
- **Relative Linking**: All links between files are relative, meaning your "brain" stays intact even if you move the root folder.

## Multi-Keep Support
You can create multiple Keeps for different areas of your life (e.g., `Work`, `Personal`, `Open-Source`). Citadel allows you to switch between them instantly, keeping your contexts isolated.
